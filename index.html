About Me

I grew up on a farm, where I learned the value of hard work, attention to detail, and bringing creative solutions to everyday challenges. There has never been a moment where I am unthankful for how I was raised. That foundation shaped how I approach design and development today. I earned my Associate's degree while balancing work and passion projects, exploring everything from web design to logo creation. Now, I am pursuing my Bachelor's degree while continuing to build my portfolio and develop my skills in both design and front-end development.

My passion projects have been the best teachers. I have designed websites with creativity, created brand identities through logo design, and made posters for local businesses. Every project has taught me something new about my dreams. I love turning ideas into thoughtful digital experiences and work that feels personal and an expression. Whether I am building a layout from scratch, or helping someone tell their story online, I bring the same care and precision that I learned growing up to every single project.
